                    Optical core after
                    Liquid Glass in the
                        Browser: Refraction with CSS and SVG
                    — kube.io, 4 September 2025.
                

                

                    That article works out the refraction field and encodes it as an
                    image, red channel for X and green for Y, so
                    feDisplacementMap can bend the backdrop. This page
                    bakes the same maps and reads them back with
                    xChannelSelector="R" and
                    yChannelSelector="G".
                

                

                    折射核心參考 kube.io 的〈Liquid Glass in the Browser〉一文:
                    將折射位移場烘焙成貼圖,紅色通道對應 X 軸、綠色通道對應 Y 軸,
                    再交給 feDisplacementMap 彎折背景。
